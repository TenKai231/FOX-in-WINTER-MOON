Project Description

Fox. is a web-based application that lets users explore and listen to various animal sounds, with a focus on red fox vocalizations (Vulpes vulpes). The project aims to educate users about the diversity of animal calls and raise awareness of wildlife conservation.

Our Mission

Bridging Nature & Technology

We believe the web can serve as a bridge between modern humans and the natural world. Through data visualization, interactive audio, scientific photography, and cinematic narration — we bring the wonder of Vulpes vulpes directly to your screen. Every page is crafted as a doorway into a deeper understanding of ecosystems and wildlife behaviour.
