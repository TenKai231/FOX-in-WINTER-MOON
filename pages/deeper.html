FOX FACTS

Categorical Data / 01
⚡

Speed

Red foxes can reach speeds of up to 30 mph (50 km/h) in short bursts, allowing them to evade larger predators.

👂

Hearing

Their hearing is so sensitive they can hear rodents scurrying underground or beneath deep snowpacks.

🌿

Survival

Adaptable omnivores, foxes survive in environments ranging from the high Arctic to urban city centers.

🧬

Subspecies

Over 40 distinct subspecies exist, each uniquely evolved for its specific local ecological niche.

🌙

Activity

Primarily nocturnal or crepuscular, they are most active during the twilight hours of dawn and dusk.

🌍

Range

Foxes have the widest natural distribution of any land mammal except for humans themselves.

THE GLOBAL FAMILY

Three distinct paths of evolution across the continents.

Red Fox

Vulpes Vulpes

The largest of the true foxes and the most geographically widespread of all members of the order Carnivora.

🦊

Arctic Fox

Vulpes Lagopus

Built for extreme cold, featuring a thick white coat that changes color with the seasons for camouflage.

🦊

Fennec Fox

Vulpes Zerda

The smallest species of canid in the world, renowned for its unusually large ears used to dissipate heat.

THERMAL ADAPTATION

Foxes are masters of thermodynamic regulation. From the sub-zero tundra to the searing Sahara, their metabolic systems adjust to maintain core stability.
